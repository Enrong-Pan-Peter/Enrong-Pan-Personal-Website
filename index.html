01 · about

The short version

I’m Peter — a fourth-year student at Queen’s University double-majoring in AI and mathematics. My research lives where the two overlap: I evolve programs and study generative models, chasing AI whose reasoning a human can actually read. Right now that means working on evolutionary program synthesis for abstract reasoning in Dr. Ting Hu’s lab, with two papers in preparation.

Outside the lab I read a lot — English and Mandarin literature in roughly equal measure. I’m a Christian; when I noticed Christian students didn’t have a truth-or-dare game of their own, I built the first one.
